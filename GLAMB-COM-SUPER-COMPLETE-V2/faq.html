FAQ

Questions before your visit.

Short answers to the things patients most often want to know.

How long does a hearing evaluation take?

Plan for about 60–75 minutes for a comprehensive adult evaluation, including discussion of results.

Do I need a referral?

Many people can schedule directly, but insurance and medical requirements vary. Check your plan if coverage matters.

Should I bring my current hearing aids?

Yes. Bring the devices, charger or batteries, accessories, and any recent hearing test if available.

Can I try hearing aids before deciding?

The demo site describes in-clinic technology demonstrations. Exact trial policies would depend on the real clinic and local rules.

Do you see tinnitus patients?

Yes. Tinnitus visits focus on assessment, education, hearing status, management options, and referral when appropriate.

Do you offer custom hearing protection?

Yes. Options can include musician filters, occupational protection, sleep plugs, and swim molds.

Are the prices on this site final?

No. They are sample self-pay pricing for this demo build. Actual coverage and costs would need to be confirmed by the clinic.

Is this an emergency service?

No. Sudden hearing loss or urgent neurologic symptoms require prompt medical attention.
