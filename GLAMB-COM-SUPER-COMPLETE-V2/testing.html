Hearing testing

A complete picture, not just a beep test.

Your evaluation combines your listening history with objective and behavioral measures.

What may be included

Case history & listening goals

We review symptoms, noise exposure, medications, communication needs, and previous hearing care.

Otoscopy

A visual check of the ear canal and eardrum area before testing.

Pure-tone testing

Measures the softest tones you can hear across key frequencies for each ear.

Speech testing

Measures speech awareness and word understanding under controlled conditions.

Middle-ear measures

May include tympanometry when clinically appropriate.

After the test

You receive a clear explanation of your results and practical next steps. That may include monitoring, hearing technology, communication strategies, protection, or a medical referral.
