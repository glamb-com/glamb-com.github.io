Tinnitus care

Understand the sound. Build a practical management plan.

Tinnitus can have many patterns. Our role is to assess hearing, identify red flags that need referral, and discuss evidence-informed management options.

01

History & hearing evaluation

We review the sound, onset, patterns, hearing changes, noise exposure, sleep, and daily impact.

02

Education & sound strategies

Learn how attention, environment, and sound enrichment can influence how noticeable tinnitus feels.

03

Follow-up plan

When appropriate, we discuss hearing aids, sound therapy options, communication care, or referral.

Seek urgent medical care

Sudden hearing loss, severe dizziness, neurologic symptoms, or certain types of one-sided/pulsatile tinnitus can require prompt medical evaluation. This website is informational and is not emergency care.
